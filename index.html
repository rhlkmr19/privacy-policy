App: White Board  |  Effective date: 7 June 2026  |  Last updated: 31 October 2026

AiTricker ("we", "our", "us") built the White Board Android application (the "App") as a free product. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and the third-party services integrated within it access, collect, use, and share when you use the App. By installing or using White Board, you agree to the practices described below.

The App is provided "as is" and is intended for general drawing, teaching, and note-taking on a digital whiteboard. The App works fully offline for its core drawing features; an internet connection is required for advertisements, in-app review prompts, the optional Web Image Search feature, the optional AI-powered OCR Scanner feature, and the optional Live Class feature (Section 2.4).

2. Information We Collect

Most of White Board works only on your device. Information leaves your device only for the optional online features described below: Google sign-in (OCR Scanner, Screen Recorder Pro, Live Class subscription), the OCR Scanner, Web Image Search, in-app purchases, Live Class, and the third-party SDKs listed in Section 4 (for example, advertisements). We never sell your information.

2.1 Information stored locally on your device

  • Drawings, boards, notes and sessions you create.
  • Photos captured via the in-app camera or chosen from your gallery and inserted into a board.
  • OCR scan history — text recognized from scanned images is stored locally for your convenience (last 20 scans only).
  • Videos you play on the board — with the Video tool you choose a video file from your device with the system file picker. It is played on your device only; the App never uploads it. If you record a lesson, the picture and sound of the Video window can be part of your Screen Recorder video (below).
  • Screen Recorder videos — when you use the Record tool, the video of your board (and, if you switch them on, your voice from the microphone and your face from the front camera in a round or square bubble) is saved as a file in your device gallery (folder Movies/WhiteBoard). These videos stay on your device. The App never uploads them and never sends them to our server; they leave your device only if you choose to share them yourself.
  • App preferences (theme, default tools, last-used settings, tour completion flag, session-open count, etc.).

Locally stored data is kept on your device only using Android's built-in storage APIs, Room database and Jetpack DataStore.

2.2 Information sent to our server

When you use the OCR Scanner feature, the following data is sent to our server (a Google Cloud Function hosted on Google Cloud Platform):

  • Google account authentication: To use the OCR Scanner, you must sign in with your Google account via Firebase Authentication. The same sign-in is used by the Screen Recorder. Your Google user ID (a unique internal identifier) is sent with each OCR request, and with the purchase check of the Screen Recorder Pro purchase, to enforce per-user scan limits and verify premium purchase status. Firebase Authentication (Google) keeps the email address and name of your Google account in our Firebase project so that you can sign in; we use only the user ID on our server, we show your email address only on your own device, and we never see your password.
  • Image data: The photo you capture or select is sent to our server for AI-based text recognition. The image is processed in real-time and is not stored on the server — it is discarded immediately after processing.
  • Device identifier (Android ID): A non-resettable, per-app device identifier (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) is sent with each OCR request to prevent abuse (limiting the number of accounts per device). On our server it is linked only to the opaque user ID of the account(s) that used OCR on that device — never to your name or email address.

2.3 Information stored on our server

The following data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (a Google-managed database) to enforce scan limits and track premium status:

  • Google user ID (an opaque internal identifier — not your name or email)
  • Device identifier (Android ID)
  • Number of OCR scans used
  • Whether the user has premium status (unlocked via in-app purchase)
  • Whether the user has bought Screen Recorder Pro (a one-time in-app purchase) and its purchase token
  • Whether the user has bought Remove Ads (a one-time in-app purchase) and its purchase token
  • Purchase token (for verifying premium purchases with Google Play)
  • Timestamp of the first and last scan
  • For a Live Class subscription: the purchase token, its state (active, expired, ...), whether at least one period was paid, and small daily usage counters (classes started, minutes of audio and camera today)
  • A record that a purchase token has already been used by an account (the account's user ID, the product and the date), so that one purchase cannot be claimed by several accounts

No OCR images and no recognized text are stored on our server. Live Class data is described in Section 2.4 and is deleted with the class.

2.4 Live Class (teachers and students)

Live Class lets a teacher show the board live on students' phones, speak to the class and, if the teacher wants, show their face. It works only while a class is running.

  • Teachers: To start a class you need a Live Class subscription bought through Google Play with your Google account. We send to our servers the class name you type, a 6-digit class code, and what you show on your board during the class (pen strokes, shapes, text, page changes, the visible area of the board, the laser pointer, the board colour and the geometry tools). PDF pages you open are sent as compressed pictures (at most 300 KB each) so that students can see them. If you use "Show to class", a picture of that student's board is sent to the class, without the student's name unless you choose "Show name".
  • Teacher's microphone and camera: If you turn on the mic, your voice is sent live to the students of the class. If you turn on the face cam, a video of your face is sent live to them. The App asks before it uses the microphone or the camera, and you can turn either off at any time. The voice and the video are not recorded or stored by us; they are relayed in real time (see "Who processes the data" below). If you leave the App for a moment during the class (for example to pick a PDF file), the mic and the face cam you turned on keep working, and Android shows a "Live class is on" notification while they do; they stop when you turn them off or end the class. The App tells you this before it turns them on.
  • Students: To join a class you type the class code and a name (your first name or a roll number is enough). You need no account. Only your teacher sees your name, unless your teacher calls you to the board (your name is shown next to what you write) or shows your work to the class with your name. We also send whether you are connected, when you were last active, and a raised hand. If your teacher lets you draw on your own board, only your teacher sees it. If your teacher calls you to the board, the whole class sees what you write.
  • Student's microphone: While you are on the board your microphone is on, and your teacher and the class hear you. The App tells you this and asks before it uses the microphone. Your voice is not recorded or stored by us. Students' phones never send video. The QR scanner is Google's code scanner in Google Play services.
  • Students' class recordings: If a student uses Record in a class, what the student sees on the class board, the teacher's face cam and voice (and the student's own voice while on the board) are saved as a video in the gallery of that student's phone (folder Movies/WhiteBoard). The video stays on that phone. The App never uploads it and we never receive it. Teachers agree to this in the Terms (section 4A).
  • Anonymous identifier: Every phone that uses Live Class gets an anonymous identifier from Firebase Authentication (no name, email or phone number), so that our servers can tell the phones of a class apart. An identifier that is not used for 30 days is deleted.
  • Who processes the data and where: Google Firebase stores the class data for us: the live data in Firebase Realtime Database in Singapore (we may add databases in the United States and Belgium so that classes far from Asia are faster; a class then stays in the database nearest its teacher) and the pictures in Cloud Storage in Mumbai, India. The live voice and video are relayed by Cloudflare's network (Cloudflare Realtime) from the city nearest each phone; Cloudflare processes them only to deliver them. Firebase and Cloudflare also see technical data needed to connect, such as the IP address. Both act as our service providers.
  • How long: A class and everything in it (names, board data, pictures) is deleted from our servers when the teacher ends it, or automatically 15 minutes after the teacher's phone stops connecting, and in any case after at most 12 hours. Pictures are deleted at the latest one day after upload. Live voice and video are never stored. The teacher's board stays on the teacher's phone in History like any other board.
  • Reports: In the latest version, a student can report the class or its teacher from inside the class. A report keeps the reason and the optional note, the class name and code, the name the student uses in the class, the anonymous identifiers of the student and the teacher and, for a paid class, the teacher's account user ID. The teacher is not told who sent a report. We keep a report for 90 days to look at it, then it is deleted; we may stop a class, refuse new classes or block an account that breaks the Terms.
  • We do not use class data for advertising or profiling, we do not sell it, and we show no ads anywhere in Live Class.

The App includes a Web Image Search feature that allows you to search for freely-licensed images and insert them into your whiteboard. When you use this feature, your search query is sent to the Wikimedia Commons public API to retrieve matching images.

  • Data sent: Only the text you type as a search query is transmitted. No personal information, device identifiers, or location data is sent with the request.   • Data received: Image thumbnails, URLs, titles and attribution metadata from Wikimedia Commons.
  • Storage: Downloaded images are stored locally on your device as part of the board. They are not uploaded to any server.

This feature is entirely optional and only activates when you explicitly tap "Web Image" and enter a search query. The Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy applies to requests made to their API:
https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Privacy_policy

4.4 Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In)

The App uses Firebase Authentication to sign you in with your existing Google account when you use the OCR Scanner feature. The Google account sheet you see is shown by Google (the Android Credential Manager) on your device; the App only receives the resulting Google ID token.

  • Data accessed: Firebase Authentication receives a Google ID token from Google Sign-In and keeps your Google account's email address and name in our Firebase project so that you can sign in.   • Data stored: The Firebase UID is stored in our Firestore database as the record key for scan-count and premium-status tracking (see Section 2.3).
  • Sign-out: You can sign out of the App at any time. Your locally stored drawings and boards are not affected by sign-out.
  • Delete your account: You can delete your account and the data our server keeps for it from inside the App (see Section 8.1).

The App uses Firebase App Check with the Play Integrity API to verify that OCR, purchase and Live Class requests originate from a genuine, unmodified installation of the App. This helps protect our server from automated abuse.

  • Data sent: An App Check token (generated by the Play Integrity API) is sent with each OCR request. This token attests that the request comes from a legitimate app instance — it does not contain any personal information.
  • Data stored: App Check tokens are not stored on our server. They are verified in real-time and discarded.

4.8 AI-Powered OCR Scanner (Google Cloud Functions & Vertex AI)

The App includes an optional OCR Scanner feature that uses artificial intelligence to recognize handwritten or printed text from images. When you use this feature:

  • Image processing: The image you capture or select is sent over HTTPS to a Google Cloud Function hosted on Google Cloud Platform (region: asia-south1). The Cloud Function forwards the image to Google Vertex AI (Gemini model) for text recognition. The image is processed in real-time and is not stored on our server or by Vertex AI after processing.
  • Device identifier: Your device's Android ID is sent with the request to enforce the free scan limit. See Section 2.2 for details.
  • Data returned: Only the recognized text is returned to the App. The text is stored locally on your device in the scan history.

This feature is entirely optional and only activates when you explicitly tap "OCR" and capture or select an image. Google's Vertex AI is governed by Google Cloud's privacy policy:
https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice

8. Data Retention and Deletion

8.1 Delete your White Board account

This applies if you signed in to White Board (developer: AiTricker) with your Google account. You can delete your account and the data our server keeps for it at any time:

  • In the App (latest version): open the Home screen, tap the Settings (gear) icon, then Account → Delete account, read what will be deleted and tap Delete account. It happens at once.
  • By email: write to nonytoons2011@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with, subject "Delete my White Board account". We reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted: your sign-in (the Firebase account with your email address and name), your OCR scan record (except the used free-scan count below), OCR Premium and Screen Recorder Pro unlocks, your Live Class subscription record and its daily usage counters.

What is kept, and why:

  • A record that a purchase token was already used (the deleted account's user ID, a one-way coded form of its Google account ID, the product and the date), so that one purchase cannot be claimed again by another account (fraud prevention). It holds no name or email.
  • A record of how many free OCR scans and scan attempts a Google account used (a one-way coded form of the Google account ID and those counts), so that the free scan limit cannot be reset by deleting and re-creating the account (fraud prevention). It holds no name or email.
  • A record that a Google account already had the Live Class free trial (a one-way coded form of the Google account ID and the date), so that the free trial cannot be taken again by deleting and re-creating the account (fraud prevention). It holds no name or email.
  • The record of your phone (Android ID and the opaque user IDs that used OCR on it), which stops the free limit and the accounts-per-phone limit from being reset by deleting and re-creating an account (fraud prevention). It holds no name or email.
  • Live Class reports (Section 2.4) stay until their 90 days are over, so that we can still act on them.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records; the Remove Ads purchase comes back with "Restore purchases". OCR Premium, Screen Recorder Pro and the Live Class subscription cannot be moved to a new sign-in after the account is deleted.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel a Live Class subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions) first.
  • Server logs may keep technical entries for up to 30 days.

Delete some data without deleting the account: email us at the address above and say what to delete (for example, your OCR scan record). Live Class data is deleted automatically (Section 2.4).

8.2 Local data and other data

Local data: All user-created content (drawings, photos, sessions, scan history, preferences) is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual boards/sessions or scan history from within the App.
  • Clearing the App's data: Android Settings → Apps → White Board → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data.

Server-side data: The OCR scan count record stored in Firestore (device identifier, scan count, timestamps) is retained to enforce the free scan limit. Images sent for OCR processing are not retained — they are discarded immediately after text recognition is complete. To delete your account's server data see Section 8.1.

For data collected by AdMob, please refer to Google's data deletion mechanisms in their privacy policy.

9. Data Security

User content is stored in the App's private storage area on your device, which is protected by Android's app-sandbox model. All network traffic — including OCR image uploads, scan-limit checks, AdMob requests, Google Play services and Live Class — is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS (Live Class voice and video: WebRTC encryption). Server-side data in Firestore is protected by Google Cloud Platform's security infrastructure including encryption at rest.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
